Welcome to location.zakz.info ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information you share with us when you visit our website or use our services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect
We believe in data minimization and collect only what is necessary to operate, secure, and improve the service:

Location Data: You are not sharing location data with us.  We lookup the country that your country resolves to, and then put a placemark at the geographic center of the country that you are located in.  

Log & Technical Data: Like most websites, our web servers automatically collect standard technical information when you visit, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and timestamps. This data is used solely for security, debugging, and operational stability.

Cookies and Local Storage: We do not use essential cookies, session storage, or local storage tokens to maintain your preferences or session state. We do not use third-party tracking or advertising cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited data we collect for the following purposes:

To provide, maintain, and render map tiles, location markers, and geographical features.

To monitor system performance, diagnose technical issues, and ensure the security and integrity of the site.

To comply with applicable legal obligations if required.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell, trade, rent, or monetize your personal information or location data. We may disclose information only under the following limited circumstances:

Legal Compliance: If required by law, subpoena, or legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or the public.

Service Providers: Infrastructure providers (such as hosting or content delivery networks) that assist in operating the website, bound by confidentiality obligations.

4. Data Security
We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights and Choices
Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the GDPR or CCPA), you may have certain rights regarding your personal data:

Access and Correction: You can request details about the data we hold regarding your usage.

Opt-Out of Location Sharing: You can revoke location permissions at any time through your web browser or device settings. Doing so may limit certain interactive mapping features of the site.

Data Deletion: You may request the deletion of any stored logs or session data associated with your visits.
